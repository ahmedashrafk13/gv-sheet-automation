Format examples

Message in the sheet, with a variable

Phone,First Name,Message
+12125550147,Sam,"Hi {{First Name}}, your order is ready."
(646) 555-0123,Alex,Your appointment is at 3 pm.

Same message for everyone

Phone,First Name
+12125550147,Sam
(646) 555-0123,

With Hi {{First Name|there}}, Alex (blank name) gets “Hi there”.

  • Variables ignore case and spaces: {{first_name}} works too.
  • A “Name” column also gives {{First Name}} and {{Last Name}}.
  • Quote CSV values that contain commas.
  • Numbers without a country code are treated as US/Canada.

    Sending

    Pacing

    sec

    45 to 3,600 seconds. Default 60.

    sec

    Up to this much is added at random. 0 turns it off.

    /day

    0 means no limit. Once reached, the queue resumes after midnight.

    and

    Your computer’s time. Outside these hours the queue waits and continues by itself. The TCPA quiet hours are 9 PM to 8 AM in the recipient’s time zone.

    Safety

    1 to 10. Unconfirmed sends count as failures.

    Alerts

    A notification when a queue finishes or stops on a problem, so you don’t have to watch the panel.

    Delays lower the risk but can’t guarantee you stay within Google Voice’s limits. Google sets those and can change them.

    Sent history

    Every number you text is remembered, so re-importing a sheet only texts the new rows. Kept in this browser as one-way fingerprints, not readable numbers.
